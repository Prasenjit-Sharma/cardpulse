# Plans v2 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Five plans (Free, Starter, Plus, Pro, Unlimited) with Pulse Brief on every paid plan, and lead capture with brochures as a paid feature that the database enforces, presented in the app, the tour and the store's opening slide.

**Architecture:**
- `shared/plans.ts` stays the single price list.
- Migration `0007_plans_v2.sql` re-creates the functions it changes, from `0005` and `0006`: `_allow`, `begin_read`, `begin_brief`, `_balance`, `grant_plan`. It adds `_can_collect`, and gates lead submissions and visitor-pack writes.
- The app reads `canCollect` from the balance and locks Collect leads and What visitors get behind a preview sheet.
- Copy that names Pro as Brief's only home changes to every plan.

**Tech Stack:** Supabase Postgres (PGlite in `npm run test:db`), React 19, Node test runner, Playwright for the slide capture.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-plans-v2-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- **The price list (exact):**

  | Plan | Price | Cards | Pulse Brief |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Free | ₹0 | 20 | 3 trial, once |
  | Starter | ₹99 / ₹999 a year | 100 | 3 |
  | Plus | ₹199 / ₹1,999 a year | 250 | 5 |
  | Pro | ₹399 / ₹3,499 a year | 400 | 20 |
  | Unlimited | ₹799 / ₹6,999 a year | 1,000,000 a month (shown as "Unlimited"), fair use 200 reads a day | 30 |
  | Pass | ₹499 | 1,000 over 7 days | — |

  Packs are 50, 100 and 200 cards for ₹59, ₹99 and ₹179. Extra Briefs are 10 for ₹149, open to any paid plan. Daily brakes are 300 reads and 10 Briefs, except Unlimited at 200 reads.
- **Lead capture** (collect-leads submissions, visitor-pack rows and files) is allowed only with an active paid plan or an active pass. Publishing a card is never gated, because card links stay free.
- **When a plan or pass lapses,** received leads are kept and new submissions are refused with `not_collecting`. The visitor sees "This stall isn't collecting details right now."
- **Subscribing to any paid plan** ends the 3 trial Briefs, extending 0006's rule from Pro to all paid plans.
- **The Brief refusal code** for a free account with no trial left becomes `plan_needed` ("Pulse Brief comes with every plan."), replacing `pro_only`. The Worker, SQL and app change together.
- **Nothing can be bought until Play Billing.** The listing test that forbids advertising purchases stays green, and the slide is prepared but not mentioned in `listing.json`.

## Review Focus

- A lapsed plan still accepting leads, or a paid account refused. `_can_collect` must use the same period test as `_period`. Tested in PGlite.
- Old balances cached on the phone with tier `plus` and no `canCollect`: `parseBalance` must accept them (treat `canCollect` as false). Task 3 test.
- A visitor's submission to an unentitled stall storing anything. Tested: no row in `leads`.
- Unlimited showing "1000000 left". The app must show "Unlimited". Task 3 and Task 4.
- The trial ending only for Pro. It must end for every paid tier. Tested.
