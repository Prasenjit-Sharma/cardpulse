Delete your Pulse account and data

Pulse · Business Card Reader

Delete the account (everything with it)

  1. Open Pulse and go to Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This removes, from our servers: your account, synced contacts and card photos, published card links, details sent to you that have not yet arrived, files added to your events, and your plan, packs and their record. It happens at once. Contacts already on your phone stay there until you delete them (below).

Delete only the cloud copy, keep the account

Settings → Account → Delete cloud data removes synced contacts and photos, published cards and any details not yet received. Your phone keeps its own copy. Your plan, packs and their record are kept, since they are what was paid for.

Delete what is on this phone

Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the app. Without an account, nothing is kept on our servers.

Can't open the app?

Write to us from the email address you signed in with and we will delete the account for you.
